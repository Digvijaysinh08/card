Dear Yuvika,

First of all, HAPPIEST BIRTHDAYYYY! 🥹❤️

Aa letter lakhva betho chhu toh samajh nathi padti kya thi start karu. Kemke ketlak feelings evi hoy chhe je words ma aavta j nathi... ane tu e feelings no ek bahu moto hisso chhe.

Aaje taro birthday chhe, pan mara mate aa fakt ek date nathi. Aaj na divase duniya ma ek evi person aavi, jene mali ne life thodi vadhare soft, thodi vadhare sundar lage chhe.

Kyarek khabar j nathi padti ke koi kyare aapdi life ma "bas ek vyakti" mathi "bahu important vyakti" bani jaay chhe. Tu e j vyakti chhe, Yuvika.

Mari ek dar hamesha rahe chhe, ke life busy thashe, responsibilities vadhshe, loko badlashe, raste juda thashe... Maybe aapde roj vaat pan na kari shakiye. Pan please aa vaat yaad rakhje, tu mara dil ma ek khaas jagya e hamesha rehse. Time ane distance e jagya kyarey nahi laI shake.

Ane kyarek jyare tane lage ke tu ekli chhe, ke koi tane samjatu nathi, ke tu pote ochhi chhe... tyare bas ek vaar yaad karje ke koi chhe je tane tari badhi imperfections sathe pan "enough" nahi, "ghanu badhu" maane chhe.

You don't have to be perfect to be special.
Tu jevi chhe, evi j already khaas chhe. ❤️

Tu aa badhu deserve kare chhe: genuine happiness, smile karvana endless reasons, darek kaam ma success, eva loko je tane sachche samje ane value kare, ane tara badha dreams reality bane. 🌙

Ane haan, kyarek hu tane irritate karu, stupid jokes maru, faltu ni bakbak karu... toh thodu tolerate kari leje 😂. Kemke e pan aapdi friendship no ek cute bhaag chhe.

Thank you for every small conversation, every laugh, every silly moment. Thank you for listening, for understanding, ane sauthi vadhare, mari life no ek part banva mate. 🫶

Mari pase perfect words nathi, pan aa nani website banavta banavta dil ma ek j vichar hato: "Yuvika ne khabar padvi joiye ke e mara mate ketli matter kare chhe."

Aajno divas bas taro chhe. Khub smile karje, cake khaje, photos padavje, tara loko sathe time spend karje ane yaadgar memories banavje.

Once again, happiest birthday, Yuvika! 🎂❤️
